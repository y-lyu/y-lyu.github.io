About Me

Welcome to my homepage! I'm a 1st year Ph.D. student in mathematics at NUS, having the great privilege to be advised by Prof. Xin. T. Tong. Currently, my primary research interest lies in the mathematical aspects of data science and applied probability, with an recent emphasize on theory and algorithms of diffusion models.


I am also broadly intrigued by math/physics/cs and the cutting edge and near-term technologies.

I strive to see things both in the eye of mathematics and programming.

On the mathmatical side, I enjoy using the tool of probability, analysis and geometry to understand data.

On the programming side, I like to think of aspects like data flow, memory and the construction of computing systems. I mainly write in Python, Julia, C++, and CUDA.

Feel free to reach out if you'd like to discuss any of these topics! You can call me Yang, which sounds like "young" in English.

Bio

Aug. 2024 - , Ph.D. student, Department of Mathematics, National University of Singapore, Singapore.

This is a new journey I have just embarked on. Expect for periodic updates.

Sep. 2020 - Jul. 2024, B.Sc. in Information and Computing Sciences (Honors Track), School of Mathematics, Peking University, Beijing, China.

Initially, I wanted to study pure math, especially the area of geometry. However, I found myself irresistably attracted to applied mathematics, especially machine learning and the mathematical aspect of data science. I was fortunate to be selected by a highly selective elite undergraduate training program in applied math and statistics.

During undergrad, I was very fortunate to be guided by Prof. Bin Dong and explored some prototypical ideas like the possibility of using neural nets to infer PDE dynamics from irregular and noisy observations, and the possibility of "conditioning" the neural nets such that they lie in certain subspace of Sobolev spaces (e.g. quotient space of some symmetry groups), together with Bowen Shi.

I wrote my undergrad thesis under the kind guidance of Prof. Daniel Zhengyu Huang on the topic of diffusion-model-inspired-MCMC algorithms, who introduced me to the wonderful world of sampling and generative models.

Sep. 2014 - Jul. 2020, Middle & High School, Beijing National Day School, China.

I spent six wonderful years there learning different subjects and made many life-long friends.
